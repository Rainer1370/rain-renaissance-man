What I don't do

Straight answers save us both a trip. These go to a shop or a licensed trade, and I'll tell you that up front:

  • Brakes and other safety-critical steering or suspension work
  • Electrical panel work, gas lines, and refrigerant / AC recharges
  • Big renovations — the kind that need a licensed contractor and permits
  • Engine or transmission internals

Why "Renaissance Man"?

Because that's the honest description. By trade I'm an engineer — years spent on complex machines, controls, and diagnostics, where you learn to slow down, look at the evidence, and fix the actual problem instead of the loudest symptom.

Off the clock, the same hands change oil, patch drywall, and un-stick doors for the neighbors around Coram and Longwood. One person, a lot of trades, and a simple rule: do it carefully, charge it fairly, and leave it working.
